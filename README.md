# Habit System

**Author:** Jasmína Csalová
**Institution:** Faculty of Information Technology, Brno University of Technology (FIT VUT)

**Habit System** is a cross-platform mobile application designed to help users build and maintain healthy daily habits. Built with React Native and Expo, the app combines habit tracking with social and gamification elements to keep users motivated and engaged on their self-improvement journey.

Users can create personalised daily routines, monitor their progress over time, participate in challenges with friends, and earn rewards — all within a clean, intuitive interface.

## Key Features

- **Daily Routine Tracking** — Create, organise, and reorder custom daily routines with drag-and-drop support. Track completion with visual progress indicators.
- **Challenges** — Set personal challenges with configurable durations (1–365 days) and invite other users to participate. Completed challenges are marked with a celebratory badge.
- **Community Feed** — Browse and interact with posts from other users, filtered by tags and activity.
- **Insights & Statistics** — View detailed analytics including streak data, success rates, and a performance index that evaluates consistency over time.
- **Rewards & Shields** — A gamification layer where users earn shields for consistent habit completion and unlock rewards.
- **Push Notifications** — Configurable reminders and in-app notifications powered by Expo Notifications and Firebase.
- **Google Sign-In** — Secure, one-tap authentication via Google.
- **Cloud Sync** — All data is persisted in Firebase, ensuring seamless access across devices.

## Gamification Design

The app's gamification mechanics are designed around the **Octalysis framework** by Yu-kai Chou. All **four dimensions** of the framework are utilised:

- **White Hat / Left Brain** — Epic Meaning & Calling, Development & Accomplishment (routine streaks, performance index, progress visualisation)
- **White Hat / Right Brain** — Empowerment of Creativity & Feedback, Social Influence & Relatedness (community feed, challenges with friends)
- **Black Hat / Left Brain** — Ownership & Possession, Scarcity & Impatience (shields, rewards, limited-duration challenges)
- **Black Hat / Right Brain** — Unpredictability & Curiosity, Loss & Avoidance (streak protection, notification reminders)

This ensures a balanced motivational design that drives both intrinsic and extrinsic engagement.
